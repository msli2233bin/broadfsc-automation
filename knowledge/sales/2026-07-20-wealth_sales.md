**Customer Communication Scripts (Adaptable)**

1. **Introduction script**:
"Hello, my name is [Your Name] and I'm a financial advisor at [Your Firm]. I'm reaching out because I understand you're looking for a trusted advisor to help you achieve your financial goals. Can we schedule a call to discuss how we can help?"
2. **Needs assessment script**:
"Can you tell me a little bit about your current financial situation and what you're hoping to achieve in the next [X] months/years? This will help me understand your needs and provide you with tailored advice."
3. **Solution presentation script**:
"Based on our conversation, I recommend [solution/product/service]. This will help you [achieve specific goal]. I'd be happy to walk you through the details and answer any questions you may have."
4. **Closing script**:
"Based on our discussion, I believe we can help you achieve your financial goals. Would you like to move forward with our services? If so, I'll provide you with a proposal outlining the details and next steps."
