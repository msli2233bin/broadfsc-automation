**Customer Communication Scripts (Adaptable)**

Here are some adaptable customer communication scripts based on the content from Gong Labs:

1. **Discovery Call Script**:
	* Introduction: "Hi [Executive], I'm [Your Name] from [Your Company]. I'm reaching out to learn more about [Their Company] and how we can help."
	* Ask questions: "Can you tell me more about [Their Company]'s goals and challenges?"
	* Summarize: "Just to make sure I understand, [Executive], you're looking for [Solution] to [Problem]. Is that correct?"
2. **Cold Email Script**:
	* Subject: "Hi [Executive], I'm [Your Name] from [Your Company]. I came across [Their Company] and thought we could help with [Solution]."
	* Body: "I'd love to schedule a call to discuss further. Can you let me know if you're interested?"
3. **Role-Specific Prompt Script**:
	* Introduction: "Hi [Executive], I'm [Your Name] from [Your Company]. I'm reaching out to discuss [Solution] for [Role]."
	* Ask questions: "Can you tell me more about [Role]'s goals and challenges?"
	* Summarize: "Just to make sure I understand, [Executive], you're looking for [Solution] to [Problem]. Is that correct?"
