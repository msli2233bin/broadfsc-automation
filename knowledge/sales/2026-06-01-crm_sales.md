**Customer Communication Scripts (Adaptable):**

1. **SPIN Selling Questions**:
	* Situation: What are your current pain points?
	* Problem: How do these pain points affect your business?
	* Implication: What are the implications of not addressing these pain points?
	* Need-Payoff: How can we help you address these pain points?
2. **Customer Feedback Loop Script**:
	* "Thank you for taking the time to share your feedback with us. Can you tell me more about what you liked/disliked about our product/service?"
	* "How can we improve our product/service to better meet your needs?"
	* "What would it take for you to become a loyal customer?"
