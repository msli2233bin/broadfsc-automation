**Platform-Specific Strategies**

### Instagram

* Build a strong Instagram community through two-way engagement, interactive Stories, user-generated content (UGC), and a consistent cadence.
* Track saves, shares, and DMs weekly to measure engagement.
* Use Instagram's features such as Reels, IGTV, and Shopping to diversify content and increase engagement.

### TikTok

* Create short-form, engaging content that resonates with the TikTok audience.
* Utilize hashtags and participate in trending challenges to increase visibility.
* Leverage TikTok's Duet feature to collaborate with other creators and expand reach.

### LinkedIn

* Share industry insights, thought leadership pieces, and company news to establish authority.
* Utilize LinkedIn's publishing platform to share long-form content and drive engagement.
* Leverage LinkedIn's Groups feature to connect with industry professionals and thought leaders.

### Facebook

* Share a mix of promotional, educational, and entertaining content to keep audiences engaged.
* Utilize Facebook's Groups feature to connect with customers and build a community.
* Leverage Facebook's advertising platform to target specific audiences and drive conversions.

### Twitter

* Share bite-sized, engaging content that resonates with the Twitter audience.
* Utilize hashtags and participate in trending conversations to increase visibility.
* Leverage Twitter's Poll feature to engage with audiences and drive conversations.
