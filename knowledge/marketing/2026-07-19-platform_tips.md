**Platform-Specific Strategies**

### Instagram

- **Best Time to Post**: According to Buffer's analysis, the best time to post on Instagram in 2026 is between 12 pm and 4 pm, with the highest engagement rate at 3 pm.
- **Post Format**: Instagram posts with images and videos perform better than those with only text.
- **Hashtags**: Use relevant and specific hashtags to increase engagement and reach a wider audience.

### TikTok

- **Best Time to Post**: Buffer's analysis suggests that the best time to post on TikTok in 2026 is between 9 am and 12 pm, with the highest engagement rate at 10 am.
- **Hashtags**: Use popular and relevant hashtags to increase engagement and reach a wider audience.
- **Content Length**: Keep TikTok videos short and engaging, ideally between 15-60 seconds.

### X (Twitter)
