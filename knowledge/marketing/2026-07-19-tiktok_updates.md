**Platform-Specific Strategies**

### TikTok

- **In-App Experiences**: Create immersive, dedicated hubs for artists or brands, featuring exclusive content, interactive tasks, and community challenges.
- **Exclusive Content**: Offer unique video content, available only on TikTok, to drive engagement and encourage users to share their experiences.
- **Community Challenges**: Launch global challenges that invite users to showcase their creativity, using a brand's music, products, or theme.
- **Profile Frames**: Unlockable profile frames can be used as rewards for completing interactive tasks or participating in community challenges.
