## 来源: TikTok Newsroom

**Platform-Specific Strategies:**

### TikTok

- **Exclusive in-app experiences**: Create unique experiences that bring fans closer to the artist and their music, such as profile frames, challenges, and curated showcases.
- **Search Hub**: Develop dedicated destinations for fans to discover new content, connect with the artist's fan community, and explore the latest album releases.
- **Fan engagement**: Encourage fans to participate in challenges, create their own content inspired by the artist, and showcase standout creations in a curated spotlight.

**Content Creation Tips:**

### Music Promotion

- **Collaborate with artists**: Partner with popular artists to create exclusive content, promote their music, and tap into their fanbase.
- **Cross-cultural content**: Create music that effortlessly crosses cultures and continents, as seen in Tyla's fusion of amapiano and pop.
- **Viral challenges**: Launch iconic dance challenges, like the #WaterChallenge, to inspire creators and turn a distinctly local sound into a global cultural phenomenon.

**Engagement Tactics:**

### Fan Interaction

- **Fan Spotlight**: Curate standout fan videos inspired by the artist and the new album to showcase creativity and community engagement.
- **Artist takeovers**: Allow artists to take over their own profiles, share behind-the-scenes content, and connect with fans in a more personal way.
- **Community building**: Foster a sense of community by creating dedicated destinations for fans to connect with each other and the artist.

**Algorithm Insights:**

### Music Discovery

- **Algorithmic curation**: Leverage TikTok's algorithm to curate content that showcases the artist's music, fan creations, and community engagement.
- **Music-driven content**: Prioritize content that features the artist's music, such as videos, challenges, and live streams, to increase visibility and engagement.
- **Global reach**: Tap into TikTok's global reach to introduce new audiences to the artist's music and promote their brand.
