**Key Concepts Explained Simply**

3. **K-Shaped Economy**: A term used to describe an economy where some sectors are growing rapidly, while others are struggling or declining, creating a "k" shape.
4. **Bear Market**: A period of significant decline in stock prices, often defined as a 20% drop from recent highs.
5. **Tariffs**: Taxes imposed on imported goods, which can impact trade and economic growth.
