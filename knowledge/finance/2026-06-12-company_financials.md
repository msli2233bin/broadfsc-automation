**Key Concepts Explained Simply:**

1. **IPO (Initial Public Offering)**: When a private company issues stocks to the public for the first time, allowing individuals to buy and own shares.
2. **Stock Market**: A platform where companies' stocks are traded, allowing investors to buy and sell shares.
3. **Futures**: A contract to buy or sell an asset (e.g., stocks, commodities) at a predetermined price on a specific date.
4. **Inflation**: A sustained increase in the general price level of goods and services in an economy over time.
5. **AI (Artificial Intelligence)**: A technology that enables machines to perform tasks that typically require human intelligence.
6. **ETF (Exchange-Traded Fund)**: A type of investment fund that tracks a specific index, sector, or asset class, traded on a stock exchange.
7. **H-1B Fee**: A visa program for foreign workers in specialty occupations, with a $100,000 fee for employers.

**Practical Trading Tips:**

1. **Diversification**: Spread investments across different asset classes, sectors, and geographic regions to minimize risk.
2. **Research**: Thoroughly research a company or investment before making a decision.
3. **Risk Management**: Set stop-loss orders and position sizing to manage risk and prevent significant losses.
4. **Stay Informed**: Stay up-to-date with market news, economic indicators, and company announcements.
5. **Long-Term Perspective**: Focus on long-term growth and avoid making impulsive decisions based on short-term market fluctuations.
