**Key Concepts Explained Simply**

### 1. Types of Stocks

- **Common Stock**: Offers high potential returns but higher risks. Shareholders have voting rights and can benefit from capital appreciation.
- **Preferred Stock**: Provides stability with fixed dividends. Shareholders have limited voting rights and priority over common stockholders in case of liquidation.
- **Domestic and International Stocks**: Stocks from companies based in the same country (domestic) or different countries (international).
- **Growth Stocks and Value Stocks**: Growth stocks have high growth potential but may be more volatile. Value stocks have lower prices relative to their intrinsic value.
- **Dividend Stocks and Non-Dividend Stocks**: Dividend stocks pay regular dividends to shareholders. Non-dividend stocks do not pay dividends.
- **Cyclical Stocks and Non-Cyclical Stocks**: Cyclical stocks are sensitive to economic cycles. Non-cyclical stocks are less sensitive to economic cycles.

### 2. Stock Market Capitalization

- **Large-Cap Stocks**: Stable and established companies with high market capitalization.
- **Mid-Cap Stocks**: Balanced risk and return, with moderate market capitalization.
- **Small-Cap Stocks**: High growth potential but higher risks, with lower market capitalization.

### 3. Diversification

- **Diversify Across Stock Types**: Spread investments across different types of stocks to mitigate risks.
- **Diversify Across Sectors**: Invest in various sectors to reduce dependence on a single industry.
- **Diversify Across Market Caps**: Invest in large-cap, mid-cap, and small-cap stocks to balance risk and return.

### 4. Stock Classes

- **Stock Classes (A, B, C shares)**: Different classes of stock with varying rights and privileges.

### 5. Research and Trends
