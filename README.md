## What This Repo Automates

| Workflow | Schedule | Action |
|----------|----------|--------|
| `peptide_promotion.yml` | Daily 02:00 UTC | Posts a research peptide to the Telegram channel |
| `peptide_channels.yml` | Daily | Multi-channel distribution (Threads/Mastodon/Discord/Bluesky/Pinterest/X/LinkedIn when credentials are valid) |
| `peptide_newsletter.yml` | Weekly | Groq-generated research briefing → Telegram + HTML |
| `peptide_seo.yml` | Daily | Regenerates the SEO catalogue and re-submits URLs to IndexNow |

All generated content enforces **Research Use Only** compliance — no therapeutic, diagnostic, or
human-consumption claims. All peptides are supplied as laboratory research chemicals.

## SEO Engine

The `docs/peptide-seo/` static site is generated by `build_peptide_seo.py` and covers:

- 20 individual research-peptide pages (with sequence, CAS, purity, research focus)
- 8 research-area category pages
- 12 country/region long-tail pages
- 10 peptide comparison pages
- 12 educational blog articles
- FAQ, RSS feed, sitemap, and JSON-LD structured data (Product / FAQPage / BreadcrumbList / BlogPosting)
